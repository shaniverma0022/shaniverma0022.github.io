Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser, so your image stays on your device.

Will a very blurry photo become perfectly clear?

The tool enlarges the image smoothly and sharpens edges, which makes soft photos look cleaner at large sizes. It cannot invent detail that was never captured, so very small or badly blurred photos improve less.

8K does not work on my phone. Why?

8K images need a lot of memory. If your device runs out, choose 4K instead, or try on a computer.

Is it free?

Yes, there is no signup and no limit on how many photos you enhance.
